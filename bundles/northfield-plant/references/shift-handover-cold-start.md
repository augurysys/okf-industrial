# Shift handover — night to day, 14 Feb

**Left by:** Joe Miller, Lead Millwright
**For:** oncoming day shift

Nothing broke overnight. One thing for the new folks, since it's the season for it.

> On cold winter mornings — below about 5 °C outside — P-101A always shows a little inlet-pressure dip right after start, while the intake-valve grease is still stiff and warming up. Give it about 90 seconds; if the pressure comes back on its own, it's the grease, not a fault. Don't trip a healthy pump inside that first minute and a half.

I've seen this pump come up cold for 24 years and it does the same thing every January. New operators panic at the low-pressure indication and reach for the trip. Wait it out first. If the pressure is still down after that and the DE bearing sounds rough, then it's worth a closer look — but nine times out of ten it's just the grease.

If you do end up escalating, log the ambient temperature and the exact start time so reliability can tell a cold-start dip from the real thing.

— Joe
