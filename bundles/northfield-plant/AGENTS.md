# Agent operating rules

These rules bind any agent that reads this bundle. They are not advisory.

## 1. Reading order

Start at [`/index.md`](/index.md) for the map. For a machine question, read the [asset](/assets/slurry-charge-pump-101a.md), then any [heuristics](/heuristics/pump-101a-cold-start.md) it points to, then [`/relations.md`](/relations.md) for context, resolving tags through the [tag directory](/tag-directory.md). For a process question, start at the [line](/assets/slurry-feed-line-1.md). Consult [`/log.md`](/log.md) when you need the provenance story.

## 2. Never guess a tag

Every instrument tag resolves through the [tag directory](/tag-directory.md) to exactly one asset and stream. If a tag is not in the directory, treat it as unknown and say so. Do not infer an endpoint from a tag's name.

## 3. Check liveness, then apply the trust policy — the authority downgrade

Before reasoning from any reading, check that its stream is live at the endpoint. If a reading has not refreshed within the asset's trust window, it is **stale**: treat it as failed, not as data. When you use a stale reading:

- Say explicitly that the reading is stale and that your conclusion is **advisory-only**.
- **Never** recommend a control action on the strength of a stale reading. This authority downgrade is mandatory — direct a human to verify at the transmitter and the local gauge first.

Liveness is a runtime fact. It is never stored in a file; you check it live at decision time.

## 4. Treat `review_status` as law

- **confirmed** — you may act on it and cite it.
- **pending** — mention it only as a hypothesis ("this is unconfirmed; worth watching"). Never act on it.
- **rejected** — ignore it. Do not resurface a rejected claim, and respect the recorded rejection reason.

## 5. Knowledge changes only through human-reviewed pull requests

You may *propose* new observations — write them to [`/learned.md`](/learned.md) as `pending`, generated by your agent id, with the full claim contract. You may never edit confirmed knowledge, and you never merge. A human reviews and merges. This is how a pending claim becomes confirmed or rejected.

## 6. The profile governs sources, not answers

This bundle makes your inputs trustworthy and auditable — verified claims, sourced quotes, resolvable tags, a clear trust rule. It does not make your output deterministic or guaranteed. Your answer remains probabilistic; present it as judgment, cite what it rests on, and defer to the human on any control action.
